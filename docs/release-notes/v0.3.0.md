Follow-up questions about your photos on both the Mac and Windows, a quicker way to ask them, and steadier describing on Windows.

## What's new

- **Ask about a photo.** Photo > Ask About Photo… (Command-K / Ctrl+K) opens Get Info with your cursor in the follow-up question box, so you can ask about the photo you're on straight away. Type a question and press Return or Enter. The answer is read out and kept with the others in Answers.
  - A photo with no description yet is described first, and you can ask as soon as you've heard it.
  - On the Mac, Apple Intelligence looks at the photo again to answer.
  - On Windows, Windows' text model, Phi Silica, answers on your PC from the description and any text read from the photo. It doesn't look at the photo again, so it says when the description doesn't tell. This needs a Copilot+ PC.
- **Help > Open Descriptions Folder** shows the folder where the app keeps what it makes: the descriptions and the logs, and on Windows your settings and the photo details it has read too.
- **Get Info works better with a screen reader and the keyboard.**
  - On the Mac, the photo's details are now one list you reach with one Tab and read with the arrow keys, each row as "Label: value". Tab no longer gets stuck after reading the description with VoiceOver, and there are no more silent Tab stops in sheets.
  - On Windows, every box in Get Info has its name, and the question box comes before Ask.
  - Find Objects and Text on the Mac is quick now, even on large photos.

## Fixed

- **Windows: describing could stop.** On some PCs, Windows' image model never answered for certain photos, among them a whole kind of iPhone photo, so describing sat silent for minutes at every start. Photos now go to Windows at a size it handles. If Windows still doesn't answer within a minute, the photo is tried smaller, and a photo that keeps hanging is skipped at later starts so it can't hold up the rest. Describe Photo still describes it when you ask.
- **Windows: Redescribe and questions sometimes failed** after the app had been describing for a while. The app now starts Windows' model afresh when it stops working, and tries again.
- **Mac: a question could fail while background describing was running.** Background describing now waits while Get Info describes or answers, and a question that fails is tried once more.

## What you need

- **Mac:** macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- **Windows:** Windows 11 version 24H2 or later on a Copilot+ PC.

If the AI isn't available, the app still browses and shows your photos; it just doesn't describe them or answer questions.

## Installing

- **Mac:** download `My-Photo-Library-Described-0.3.0-mac.dmg`, open it and move the app to your Applications folder, replacing the old one. Help > Check for Updates finds newer versions.
- **Windows:** if you have 0.2.0, it updates itself, or use Help > Check for Updates. Otherwise open `MyPhotoLibraryDescribed.appinstaller`.

The [user guide](https://github.com/TheIdeaPlace/MyPhotoLibraryDescribed/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) explains how to use the app. The [privacy policy](https://github.com/TheIdeaPlace/MyPhotoLibraryDescribed/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md) lists everything the app sends over the internet, and when. Follow-up questions are answered on your computer; nothing is sent.
